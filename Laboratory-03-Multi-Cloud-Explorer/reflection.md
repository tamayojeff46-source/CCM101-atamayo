# Mission Reflection

### 1. Which cloud platform impressed you the most?
Google Cloud Platform (GCP) impressed me the most due to its pioneering leadership in containerization and modern data processing. Having developed Kubernetes internally, Google offers an exceptionally seamless managed experience with Google Kubernetes Engine (GKE). Additionally, its advanced artificial intelligence offerings through Vertex AI and fast querying with BigQuery demonstrate incredible efficiency for modern, data-driven applications.

### 2. What similarities exist among AWS, Azure, and GCP?
Despite branding differences, all three cloud platforms share fundamental architectural principles. Each provider relies on a global infrastructure divided into geographic regions and isolated Availability Zones to ensure fault tolerance. Furthermore, they all offer core Infrastructure as a Service (IaaS) capabilities—specifically compute virtual machines, block/object storage, virtual private networking, and Identity and Access Management (IAM)—under pay-as-you-go financial models.

### 3. How do business requirements influence cloud platform selection?
Business requirements are the primary driver of cloud architecture decisions. Rather than simply picking the most popular provider, a cloud architect must evaluate factors such as budget, existing software ecosystems, regulatory compliance, performance, and technical expertise. For example, a company heavily invested in Microsoft enterprise tools benefits most from Azure, whereas an AI research startup requires the specialized GPU/TPU capabilities of GCP.

### 4. What did you learn from acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant?
Acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant taught me that technical decisions must always align with business objectives. Technology selection is not about personal bias; it requires systematically assessing client constraints—such as rapid scalability, enterprise identity federation, or strict operational budgets—and matching them to the specific strengths of each cloud platform.

### 5. How has your GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio improved after completing this mission?
My GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio has evolved from documenting basic cloud concepts into demonstrating strategic cloud analysis and decision-making skills. By adding multi-cloud research, structured comparison tables, scenario-based recommendations, and terminal evidence, my portfolio now effectively showcases my ability to analyze real-world cloud architecture problems like a professional solutions architect.
