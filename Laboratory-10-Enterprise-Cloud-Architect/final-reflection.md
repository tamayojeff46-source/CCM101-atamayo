
# Final Reflection: The Journey to Enterprise Cloud Architect

Over the course of CCM101's ten missions, my technical expertise grew from individual computing concepts to building scalable, secure, and automated enterprise architectures. Beginning with Linux fundamentals, the journey scaled up to container management, network security, and automated administration.
Mission 10 integrated these elements including virtualization, container decoupling, strict firewalls, and automated disaster recovery to demonstrate how modern cloud production systems operate. As a result, I now view resilient infrastructure as a combination of reliable data persistence, robust perimeter defense, and hands-off automated recovery.
